## What ModSentinel is

ModSentinel is an AI-assisted moderation tool that:
- Scores posts and comments using Google Gemini 2.0 Flash
- Surfaces a priority-ranked mod queue to subreddit moderators
- Takes automated actions (remove, flair, mod-mail) when configured thresholds are met

ModSentinel is a **decision-support tool**, not an autonomous decision maker. Moderators remain fully responsible for all moderation decisions made through or informed by the App.

## Moderator responsibilities

By using ModSentinel you agree to:

1. **Review AI decisions** — ModSentinel's auto-remove and auto-flair features act on probabilistic AI scores. You are responsible for reviewing the Shadow queue for false positives and restoring incorrectly removed content promptly.

2. **Set appropriate thresholds** — The default auto-remove threshold (95/100) is intentionally conservative. Lowering it increases the risk of false positives. You bear responsibility for threshold configuration.

3. **Comply with Reddit's rules** — All moderation actions taken through ModSentinel must comply with [Reddit's Moderator Code of Conduct](https://www.redditinc.com/policies/moderator-code-of-conduct) and [Reddit's Content Policy](https://www.redditinc.com/policies/content-policy).

4. **Provide your own Gemini API key** — You are responsible for the API key you supply, its associated costs beyond the free tier, and compliance with [Google's AI Studio Terms of Service](https://ai.google.dev/terms).

5. **Not use ModSentinel to target protected groups** — Do not configure the watchlist or subreddit rules in ways that discriminate based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics.
